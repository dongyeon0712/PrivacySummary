Privacy Policy

Last updated March 3, 2025 · Sample text

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, email, phone number, and birthday. We also collect videos, photos, and comments you post, and your contacts if you choose to sync them. Automatically, we collect your device model, IP address, and approximate location from your IP address.

2. How we use your information

We use information to run the service, personalize your feed, and measure ads. We use what you watch and search to decide which ads you see. You can turn off personalized recommendations in settings.

3. Who we share it with

We share information with service providers who work for us, with advertising partners, and with other users based on your settings. We may share your data with companies in our corporate group. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

4. Your choices and rights

You can download a copy of your data, make your account private, and delete your account from settings. Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights to access or correct your information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is active. Some records are kept for up to 18 months after you delete your account.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. We will notify you of material changes in the app, and continued use means you accept them.
